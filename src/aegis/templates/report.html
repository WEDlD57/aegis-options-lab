Inspect the system, not just the P&L.

  1. Start before the volatility shock. Select a contract and inspect its resting quotes.
  2. Jump to the event. Compare AEGIS, Symmetric, No hedge, and Delayed on exactly the same external tape.
  3. Read the audit and stress the remaining inventory. A cancellation takes time; a risk latch does not flatten positions.

All policies share the same position limits and loss/freshness controls. Quotes and risk decisions use delivered observations. The chart uses venue marks for evaluation.
